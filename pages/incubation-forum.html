About the Forum

Where ideas take flight

The SDCET Incubation Forum is the startup and innovation hub of S.D. College of Engineering & Technology, Muzaffarnagar. It brings students, faculty, alumni and local entrepreneurs together under one roof — giving early-stage ideas the space, mentorship and connections they need to grow into real ventures.
