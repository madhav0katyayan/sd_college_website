Electrical Engineering

Electrical Engineering is a core discipline that deals with the generation, transmission, distribution and utilization of electrical power, along with control systems, electrical machines and automation. The department prepares students to understand and work with the energy systems that power modern industry and infrastructure.

The department emphasizes practical training alongside classroom teaching, encourages students to take up research-based projects and MOOC/NPTEL courses for value addition, and works to build linkages with industry for real-world exposure in emerging areas of electrical engineering.

Our Vision

To prepare students with the necessary technical knowledge, practical skills, and professional expertise required to address real-time challenges in the rapidly growing field of Electrical Engineering, while promoting research in emerging areas. Our goal is to cultivate technically skilled, innovative, and ethically grounded electrical engineers who can solve real-world energy challenges and drive technological progress.

Our Mission

  • To impart teaching of electrical engineering with emphasis on recent advancements duly supported by practical training.
  • To encourage students to undertake research-based projects in emerging areas.
  • To establish linkages with industry for exposure to industrial applications.
  • To encourage students to pursue MOOC – NPTEL online courses for value addition.
  • To encourage students to acquire knowledge through internship in emerging areas.
  • To foster excellence in Electrical Engineering education and research by nurturing technically competent, innovative, and ethically driven engineers who can solve global energy challenges and accelerate technological growth.

PEOs, POs and PSOs

PEO1 : To build the soundest professional career and/or be capable enough to further acquire higher education by attaining sufficient knowledge, understanding, and practical skills in the domain.

PEO2 : To comprise exemplary attitude, communication skills, teamwork, and administration in their occupation and adjust to lifelong learning.

PEO3 : To include vast academic and experimental knowledge of fundamentals to enable them to use it to expand the enterprise and community in general.

PEO4 : Emerging as leaders, entrepreneurs, or innovators in their field.

PEO5 : Practicing professional ethics and communicating effectively in diverse teams.

Engineering Graduates will be able to

PO1 : Engineering Knowledge: Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.

PO2 : Problem Analysis: Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences.

PO3 : Design/Development of Solutions: Design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet the specified needs with appropriate consideration for public health and safety, and the cultural, societal, and environmental considerations.

PO4 : Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems: Use research-based knowledge and research methods including design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of the information to provide valid conclusions.

PO5 : Modern Tool Usage: Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools including prediction and modeling to complex engineering activities with an understanding of the limitations.

PO6 : Engineer and Society: Apply reasoning informed by contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to professional engineering practice.

PO7 : Environment and Sustainability: Understand the impact of professional engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts, and demonstrate the knowledge of, and need for sustainable development.

PO8 : Ethics: Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics and responsibilities and norms of engineering practice.

PO9 : Individual and Team Work: Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams, and in multidisciplinary settings.

PO10 : Communication: Communicate effectively on complex engineering activities with the engineering community and with society at large, such as being able to comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, make effective presentations, and give and receive clear instructions.

PO11 : Project Management and Finance: Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the engineering and management principles and apply these to one’s own work, as a member and leader in a team, to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments.

PO12 : Life-long Learning: Recognize the need for and have the preparation and ability to engage in independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change.

Engineering Graduates will be able to

PSO1 : Graduates can analyze, design, model, and maintain complex core electrical hardware, including power generation, transmission, distribution networks, control systems, and electrical machinery.

PSO2 : Graduates can apply modern simulation, computing, and automation tools — such as MATLAB, PLC automation, SCADA, and computer programming — to design, test, and troubleshoot advanced electrical and electronic systems.

PSO3 : Graduates can implement sustainable practices by designing energy-efficient systems, managing power audits, and integrating renewable energy sources to solve modern industry problems while adhering to environmental needs.

Admission Procedure & Seat Intake

1
Apply through UPTAC / AKTU

First-year admission is through UPTAC counselling based on the qualifying entrance exam; lateral entry follows AKTU’s direct-admission norms.

2
Document Verification

Submit academic records and category certificates (if applicable) for verification at the counselling centre or admission office.

3
Seat Allotment & Fee Payment

On seat allotment, confirm admission by paying the prescribed fee within the schedule issued by UPTAC / AKTU.

4
Reporting at SDCET

Report to the department with original documents for final admission confirmation, ID card and orientation.

Seat Intake

As per AICTE’s Extension of Approval (EoA) for 2026-27, the sanctioned intake for B.Tech Electrical Engineering is 30 seats.

Category-wise reservation follows the Uttar Pradesh Government / UPTAC counselling norms.
