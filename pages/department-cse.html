Computer Science and Engineering

Established in 1997, the Department of Computer Science and Engineering has been providing quality education with a strong foundation in both theoretical and practical aspects of computer hardware and software. Over the years, the department has consistently achieved academic excellence and continues to prepare students for successful careers in the rapidly growing field of technology.

The department focuses on delivering high-quality technical education through an effective teaching-learning process and experienced faculty members. Most faculty members are doctorate and postgraduate degree holders with expertise in areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Networking, Big Data, and Cyber Security. Faculty members are also actively involved in research, publications, and academic activities.

To enhance students’ technical and professional skills, the department regularly organizes workshops, webinars, conferences, expert lectures, and industry-oriented training programs. The department takes pride in its successful alumni who are working in reputed organizations such as TCS, Wipro, Infosys, Amazon, Google, Adobe, Cognizant, and many more.

The department provides modern laboratories, a dedicated project lab, and high-speed internet facilities to support practical learning and research activities. Students have access to well-equipped computer labs from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM, helping them improve their technical knowledge, communication skills, and placement readiness.

Our Vision

To empower students with globally competitive skills in Computer Science and Engineering through cutting-edge technology, interdisciplinary learning, and industry collaboration, leading to well-qualified engineers who are innovative in their profession, socially responsible, and aligned with evolving technological advancements.

Our Mission

The mission of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering is to equip, guide and inspire students to excel professionally and grow continuously in their engineering careers. This is accomplished by:

  • Providing a conducive learning environment and modern facilities that ensure a strong foundation in engineering principles, preparing students for diverse career paths.
  • Encouraging academic excellence and innovative research to enhance innovative creativity, which strengthens the department’s regional, national and international reputation.

PEOs, POs and PSOs

PEO1 : To foster continuous learning and professional development to adapt to emerging technologies and address evolving challenges in the field of computing.

PEO2 : To nurture professional competence, ethical responsibility, teamwork, and effective communication while contributing to the technological and organizational needs of society.

Engineering Graduates will be able to

PO1 : Engineering Knowledge: Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals and engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.

PO2 : Problem Analysis: Analyse a problem & identify the computing requirements appropriate to its solutions.

PO3 : Design/Development of Solutions: Design and implement hardware & software systems, components, process or program to meet the desired needs with appropriate consideration for the public health and safety, and the cultural, societal, and environmental considerations.

PO4 : Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems: Use research-based knowledge and research methods into design and conduct new experiments, analysis and interpretation of data to provide valid conclusions & recommendation.

PO5 : Modern Tool Usage: Create, Select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources and modern engineering and IT tools including prediction and modelling to complex engineering activities with an understanding of the limitations.

PO6 : The Engineer and Society: Apply reasoning informed by the contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to the professional engineering practice.

PO7 : Environment and Sustainability: Integrate computer-based solutions in environmental contexts, demonstrate the knowledge of, and need for sustainable development.

PO8 : Ethics: Demonstrate the knowledge of professional ethics and responsibilities and norms of the engineering practice.

PO9 : Individual and Team Work: Demonstrate leadership and ability to work to accomplish a common goal as an individual, and as a members or leader in diverse teams, and in multi-disciplinary settings.

PO10 : Communication: Demonstrate effective communication skills both oral and written with range of audiences.

PO11 : Project Management and Finance: Apply the knowledge and understanding of the engineering and management principles to design plan & budget and propose IT projects for an identified need within a specific scope.

PO12 : Life-long Learning: Develop confidence to acquire new knowledge in the computing discipline and to engage life-long learning.

Engineering Graduates will be able to

PSO1 : Graduates of the program will demonstrate a commitment to continuous learning and the ability to develop effective solutions to emerging challenges in the field of computing.

PSO2 : Graduates of the program will be able to apply software engineering principles and practices to design and develop effective solutions for technology-based services.

Admission Procedure & Seat Intake

1
Apply through UPTAC / AKTU

First-year admission is through UPTAC counselling based on the qualifying entrance exam; lateral entry follows AKTU’s direct-admission norms.

2
Document Verification

Submit academic records and category certificates (if applicable) for verification at the counselling centre or admission office.

3
Seat Allotment & Fee Payment

On seat allotment, confirm admission by paying the prescribed fee within the schedule issued by UPTAC / AKTU.

4
Reporting at SDCET

Report to the department with original documents for final admission confirmation, ID card and orientation.

Seat Intake

As per AICTE’s Extension of Approval (EoA) for 2026-27, the sanctioned intake for B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering is 180 seats.

Category-wise reservation follows the Uttar Pradesh Government / UPTAC counselling norms.
