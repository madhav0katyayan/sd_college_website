Artificial Intelligence

Established in 2022, the Department of Artificial Intelligence is a pioneer department conveying technical education of high quality to undergraduate aspirants across the country. It has been attracting bright students for quality education in Artificial Intelligence. Each faculty member is distinguished and venerable in their field, dedicated to working in a result-oriented manner. All the versatile faculty members of the department hold commendable and exemplary academic credentials, with ample teaching and research experience at senior levels in leading institutions of international repute. The Artificial Intelligence Department produces eminent engineers for different platforms of Information Technology, as per MNC requirements.

The department has excellent facilities and faculty members who have always been a great strength and backbone right from the beginning, achieving new goals every year. Artificial Intelligence faculty at SDCET have a broad range of educational backgrounds and scholarly interests, and bring their research expertise into the classroom through professional practice. Faculty members also serve on editorial boards / as reviewers of many prestigious journals. All labs are equipped with the latest equipment, software and high-speed internet facility round the clock — computers are loaded with the necessary software for students to practice and improve their technical skills, preparing them for placement activities. A dedicated AI Lab is also provided with 200 Mbps leased-line connectivity for student internet usage, available from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM.

Our Vision

To nurture globally competent and innovative professionals in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence through quality education, advanced technologies, interdisciplinary learning, and industry collaboration, empowering them to develop ethical and intelligent solutions for emerging technological and societal challenges.

Our Mission

The mission of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (Artificial Intelligence) is to nurture and empower students with sound knowledge of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, enabling them to build professional competence, foster innovation, and respond effectively to rapidly evolving technologies. This is accomplished by:

  • Providing quality education, modern infrastructure, and a conducive learning environment to develop strong technical and AI skills.
  • Promoting innovation, research, and interdisciplinary learning to develop intelligent and ethical solutions to real-world challenges.
  • Fostering industry interaction and practical learning through projects, internships, workshops, and collaborations.

PEOs, POs and PSOs

PEO1 : To nurture students to have successful careers by applying computer science and artificial intelligence knowledge to analyze, design, and develop innovative solutions to real-world problems.

PEO2 : To demonstrate professional competence in AI, machine learning, and emerging technologies using modern tools to develop efficient and data-driven applications.

Engineering Graduates will be able to

PO1 : Engineering Knowledge: Apply the knowledge of mathematics, science, engineering fundamentals, and an engineering specialization to the solution of complex engineering problems.

PO2 : Problem Analysis: Identify, formulate, review research literature, and analyze complex engineering problems reaching substantiated conclusions using first principles of mathematics, natural sciences, and engineering sciences.

PO3 : Design/Development of Solutions: Design solutions for complex engineering problems and design system components or processes that meet the specified needs with appropriate consideration for the public health and safety, and the cultural, societal, and environmental considerations.

PO4 : Conduct Investigations of Complex Problems: Use research-based knowledge and research methods including design of experiments, analysis and interpretation of data, and synthesis of the information to provide valid conclusions.

PO5 : Modern Tool Usage: Create, select, and apply appropriate techniques, resources, and modern engineering and IT tools including prediction and modeling to complex engineering activities with an understanding of the limitations.

PO6 : The Engineer and Society: Apply reasoning informed by the contextual knowledge to assess societal, health, safety, legal and cultural issues and the consequent responsibilities relevant to the professional engineering practice.

PO7 : Environment and Sustainability: Understand the impact of the professional engineering solutions in societal and environmental contexts, and demonstrate the knowledge of, and need for sustainable development.

PO8 : Ethics: Apply ethical principles and commit to professional ethics and responsibilities and norms of the engineering practice.

PO9 : Individual and Team Work: Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader in diverse teams, and in multidisciplinary settings.

PO10 : Communication: Communicate effectively on complex engineering activities with the engineering community and with society at large, such as, being able to comprehend and write effective reports and design documentation, make effective presentations, and give and receive clear instructions.

PO11 : Project Management and Finance: Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of the engineering and management principles and apply these to one’s own work, as a member and leader in a team, to manage projects and in multidisciplinary environments.

PO12 : Life-long Learning: Recognize the need for, and have the preparation and ability to engage in independent and life-long learning in the broadest context of technological change.

Engineering Graduates will be able to

PSO1 : Graduates will be able to apply computer science and artificial intelligence principles to analyze, design, and develop intelligent solutions for real-world problems.

PSO2 : Graduates will be able to implement AI and machine learning techniques using modern tools and technologies to develop efficient, data-driven applications.

Admission Procedure & Seat Intake

1
Apply through UPTAC / AKTU

First-year admission is through UPTAC counselling based on the qualifying entrance exam; lateral entry follows AKTU’s direct-admission norms.

2
Document Verification

Submit academic records and category certificates (if applicable) for verification at the counselling centre or admission office.

3
Seat Allotment & Fee Payment

On seat allotment, confirm admission by paying the prescribed fee within the schedule issued by UPTAC / AKTU.

4
Reporting at SDCET

Report to the department with original documents for final admission confirmation, ID card and orientation.

Seat Intake

As per AICTE’s Extension of Approval (EoA) for 2026-27, the sanctioned intake for B.Tech CSE (Artificial Intelligence) is 30 seats.

Category-wise reservation follows the Uttar Pradesh Government / UPTAC counselling norms.
