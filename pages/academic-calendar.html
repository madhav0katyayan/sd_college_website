Academic Calendar — Odd Semester, Session 2026–27

The academic calendar lists the key teaching, examination and holiday dates for the Odd Semester of the 2026–27 session, as notified by the institute.

Month-wise Schedule

July 2026

Teaching Days: 05
Academic Activities
  • Registration end – III, V, VII Sem.: 21 July
  • Commencement of classes: 27 July (3rd sem. onwards)

August 2026

Teaching Days: 24
Academic Activities
  • Orientation Programme for 1st Year: 12–14 Aug.
  • Assignment 1: 21–22 Aug.
  • Attendance display (recognition of highest-attendance students): 31 Aug.
  • Meeting-I with CRs: 25 Aug.
Holidays: Independence Day – Flag Hoisting & Cultural Fest: 15 Aug.; Eid: 26 Aug.; Raksha Bandhan: 28 Aug.

September 2026

Teaching Days: 21
Academic Activities
  • Teacher’s Day Celebration: 5 Sept. (2nd half)
  • Assignment 2: 18–19 Sept.
  • Fresher Party: 19 Sept.
  • Meeting-II with CRs: 22 Sept.
  • Assignment 3: 30 Sept.
Examinations
  • Attendance / Detained list: 3 Sept.
  • Sessional Test 1: 7–10 Sept. · Marks display: 14 Sept.
  • Parent–Teacher Meeting / letter dispatch: 11 Sept.
Holidays: Janmashtami: 4 Sept.

October 2026

Teaching Days: 21
Academic Activities
  • Assignment 4 / Meeting-III with CRs: 16–17 Oct.
  • Assignment 5: 29–30 Oct.
  • IQAC Internal Audit: 30–31 Oct.
  • Diwali Celebration: 6 Nov.
Examinations
  • Attendance / Detained list: 3 Oct. · Sessional Test 2: 5–8 Oct. · Marks display: 12 Oct.
  • Mentoring / letter dispatch: 15 Oct.
  • Attendance / Detained list (PUE): 30 Oct.
Holidays: Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti: 2 Oct.; Dussehra: 20 Oct.

November 2026

Teaching Days: 10
Academic Activities
  • Meeting-IV with CRs: 6 Nov.
  • Submission of sessional marks to the University: 16 Nov.
  • Doubt-clearing classes: 16–23 Nov.
Examinations
  • Pre-University Examination (PUE): 2–6 Nov. · Marks display: 13 Nov.
  • University Examination: from 24 Nov.
Holidays: Diwali (8), Govardhan Puja, Bhai Duj: 8–11 Nov.

December 2026

University Exams continue
Academic Activities
  • University Examinations (Odd Semester) continue through the month.
Holidays: Christmas: 25 Dec.
