SDCET Alumni Association

With over 11,500+ alumni working across India and around the world, the SDCET Alumni Association connects graduates with each other and with the institute — to network, mentor, give back and strengthen the SDCET community.

  • To actively promote interaction and networking among its alumni.
  • To be a pillar of support in their hour of need.
  • To help realize the notion of giving back to the alma mater.
  • To function on a charitable basis, and to run the Association on a ‘no profit, no loss’ basis.
  • To contribute to the vision and strengthen the image of SDCET at national & international level.
  • Organizing alumni reunions as an integral part of the Alumni Association of SDCET.
