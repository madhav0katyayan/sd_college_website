About SDCET

A distinctive combination of academic excellence & professional ethics

Established in 1997 on Jansath Road, Muzaffarnagar, S.D. College of Engineering & Technology has spent over 25 years building career aspirants into industry-ready engineers and managers. Approved by AICTE and affiliated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University, Lucknow (AKTU code 083), SDCET was the first NBA-accredited institute in the region — a distinction that continues to define how the college teaches, mentors, and places its students.

Our classrooms carry mentorship from IIT and internationally-qualified professors, backed by a central library, computer centres, and dedicated learning-resource facilities across a lush green campus.
